# Plain-language results

Speculara now leads with a short finding and a next step. Calculations, eligibility rules, schema version 2 and raw numerical outputs are unchanged.

- Correlation uses movement and connection labels. Opposite movement means one measure tended to be lower when another was higher; it does not mean bad performance. Weak connections do not imply a useful direction. Small samples retain an early-finding note.
- Demand leads with recorded sales and a complete weekly comparison when available. Saves and question counts are fallback signals. Zero sales remain zero rather than falling through to saves.
- Recommendations show three actions at most by default. More ideas and original technical evidence can be opened separately.
- Charts lead with views, saves and sales when provided. Ranking tables, ratio cohorts and thresholds are under See the numbers.
- Predict leads with a dated next-day estimate and its uncertainty reminder. Model comparison and errors remain available under See the numbers.
- Sentiment displays Positive, Neutral and Negative comments and clearly says it uses a simple word check. Method limits and keyword counts remain available.
- Chat defaults to concise findings. Explicit technical questions return the full statistics; recommendation requests return one action, or up to three when more are requested.
- Reports begin with plain findings and retain technical sections and full computed JSON.

Native details/summary controls support keyboard access and start closed. Branding, colors, neumorphic surfaces, module selection and upload/report workflows remain intact.

Verification is recorded in docs/verification.md. Examples in tests use synthetic records and do not claim real-world accuracy or causal effects.
